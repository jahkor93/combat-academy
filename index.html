Weekly pass standard

1 Striking class + 1 Grappling class + 1 additional Combat class + 1 Physical Preparation class. Five total sessions earns an A week; six or more earns an S week. Every fourth campaign week is an Assessment Week.

Grappling: No-Gi, Wrestling or Open Mat.
Combat: Muay Thai, Boxing, MMA, No-Gi, Wrestling or Open Mat.
Physical Prep: S&C Circuit, Move Strong, Bags & Bells, Bags & HIIT or Kickboxing & HIIT.
